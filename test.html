Type & Button Lab

Test bench for the jewelry site. All sizes use the golden-ratio scale (1rem, 1.618rem, 2.618rem, 4.236rem). Buttons are square. Hover over everything.

Font pairs

Heading font + body/UI font. Research consistently points to a high-contrast or classical serif for the headline with a clean geometric sans for body text and buttons.
