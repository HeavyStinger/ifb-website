Frequently Asked Questions

Our Stones

What is moissanite?

Moissanite is a gemstone with exceptional brilliance and fire. It was first found in a meteorite crater, and the stones we use are grown in a laboratory. It is very hard and durable, which makes it well suited to jewelry worn every day.

What is a lab-grown diamond?

A lab-grown diamond is a real diamond. It has the same chemical makeup, hardness and sparkle as a mined diamond, but it is grown in a controlled laboratory instead of being dug from the earth.

How do moissanite and lab-grown diamonds compare?

Both are durable and brilliant. Moissanite shows more rainbow fire in the light, while a lab-grown diamond has the classic white sparkle of a diamond. Tell us what you prefer and we will help you choose.

Custom Pieces

How does the custom design process work?

It starts with a consultation about your vision, your preferred stones and the details that matter to you. Our artisans then create a detailed 3D rendering, and we refine it together until the design is right. Once you approve it, your piece is made by hand.

How do I start a custom piece?

Send us an inquiry through the form on the Custom Inquiries page. Tell us what you have in mind and we will get back to you to arrange your consultation.

Can you make a piece for an anniversary or another milestone?

Yes. Many of our pieces mark an anniversary, a milestone or a gift. Mention the occasion in your inquiry and we will keep it in mind as we design.

Care

How should I care for my jewelry?

Clean it with warm water, mild soap and a soft brush, then dry it with a soft cloth. Keep it away from harsh chemicals, and store each piece on its own so the pieces do not scratch one another.
